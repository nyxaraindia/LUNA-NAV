# 4-minute LUNA NAV 2.0 demo script

1. **Overview (20 s):** evidence and provenance first; no invented numbers.
2. **Real map (50 s):** NASA LRO imagery from Moon Trek. Click a point near a crater, press Save as new candidate. Toggle Polar plot to show true polar geometry and why imagery stretches.
3. **LOLA analysis (60 s):** pick a candidate, Retrieve NASA LOLA sample. If NASA blocks CORS, show the honest unavailable message. Never fake it.
4. **Mission lab (60 s):** traverse distance and drive time between two candidates; sun/Earth ceilings; move the weight sliders and show the ranking change. Say clearly: relative ranking, not safety.
5. **Assistant + exports (30 s):** ask "Why is slope not enough?"; copy share link; export GeoJSON.
6. **Close (20 s):** limitations and the QGIS validation plan.

## Judge-facing differentiator
A transparent, reproducible workflow from real NASA topography to a what-if decision, where every unmeasured value stays blank.
