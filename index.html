METHOD

What the application does

  1. Converts latitude/longitude to south-polar stereographic X/Y.
  2. Reads a small window from the NASA cloud-optimized GeoTIFF using HTTP byte ranges.
  3. Samples the nearest elevation cell and, when available, the corresponding LOLA slope/error products.
  4. Reports no-data, unsupported CORS, or out-of-coverage explicitly.
PROVENANCE

Inspect official source ↗
Important technical caveat

The DEM is cloud-hosted and not copied into this ZIP. Browser sampling requires the host to allow cross-origin requests and byte-range access. Product projection/georeferencing is read from the GeoTIFF metadata; if the server blocks access, no fabricated result is shown. The precomputed LOLA slope layer is reported as a product sample; local gradient is calculated only when neighbouring elevation pixels are available.

A ranking is not a safety verdict

Scores are relative among your own candidates, normalised between the best and worst of them. Slope and error come only from retrieved LOLA samples; sites without them are not scored. Sun and Earth ceilings ignore terrain horizons.

No automatic winner

Elevation and slope alone do not establish landing safety. A complete assessment needs hazard-scale imagery, roughness, illumination over time, local horizon, communication geometry, engineering constraints and quantified uncertainty.
